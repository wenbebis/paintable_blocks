My first mod!!! This adds a Paint Roller (with a more durable variant), that allows you to recolor any vanilla colored block (except banners), as well as paint the new double-sided blocks which can have 2 different colors on them.

![You make the Paint Roller with 1 Stick, 2 Copper Ingots, and 3 White Wool.](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b69569fbb9a9e232aa118434fee12b85f810477c.png)
![You make the Golden Paint Roller with 1 Gold Ingot, 2 Quartz, and 3 White Wool.](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/667c0f971d3b72a443971b3045159c43c7dabf36.png)

The double-sided blocks are made with wool or concrete panels, and the following blocks:
- All the 12 different planks (only wool panels)
- Smooth Stone, Smooth Quartz, Polished Diorite, Andesite, Tuff, Deepslate, and Blackstone (only concrete panels)

You make the panels with 4 wool/concrete respectively (with a crafting table, any wool works, only white concrete).

![Wool Panels recipe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/55491a12568c5b16cce06da5ce58ed4f7618569e.png)
![Concrete Panels recipe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b526bbc8afe2b1f34861cee489fdd4772463ab3c.png)

Note: Since the paneled blocks use tinted textures and not the actual block textures, they're not perfect matches compared to the originals, they will work with texture packs though.
